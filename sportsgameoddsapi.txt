SportsGameOdds API v2 — NFL DraftKings Markets
=================================================

Base URL: https://api.sportsgameodds.com/v2
Auth: x-api-key header

oddID format: {statID}-{playerEntityID}-{periodID}-{betTypeID}-{sideID}
  periodID: FG = full game
  betTypeID: ou = over/under, ml = moneyline, sp = spread
  sideID: O = over, U = under, H = home, A = away

Access DraftKings odds: odds[oddKey].byBookmaker.draftkings


=== GAME LINES ===

Endpoint: GET /odds?sportID=NFL&leagueID=NFL&gameID={gameID}&bookmakerID=draftkings

Markets returned per game:

  moneyline_home      Home team moneyline win y
  moneyline_away      Away team moneyline win y
  spread_home         Home team spread (e.g. -3.5) y
  spread_away         Away team spread y
  total_over          Game total over (e.g. 47.5) y
  total_under         Game total under y

=== NOTES ===

- Not all markets available for every game or player
- DraftKings availability varies; check odds[key].byBookmaker.draftkings !== undefined
- Lines returned as array of {line, overOdds, underOdds} objects
- Player entityID is the player's SportsGameOdds playerID
- Game lines oddKeys follow pattern: moneyline_home, spread_home, total_over (no playerEntityID component)
