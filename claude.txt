DECIDED
=======

- Member removal: commissioner can only remove members before season starts.
  No mid-season removal for now.

- Allow Public Fill join flow: public-fill players join without invite code
  and are immediately ACTIVE — no approval step. Invite-code joins still go
  through the normal PENDING → approval flow. Public-fill players are tracked
  separately on their membership so the system can enforce the cap.

- League deletion: commissioner can only delete the league if they are the
  sole member. Once any other member has joined, the league cannot be deleted.
